# Assessment Test Guidelines

## Purpose & Format
- The baseline assessment test checks logical reasoning, basic problem-solving, and foundational aptitude for your chosen track.
- Format: Online multiple-choice and short coding/logic questions (typically 45 to 60 minutes).
- You need a stable internet connection, a modern browser, and a quiet environment.

## Attempts, Retakes & Results
- Most applicants get 1 primary attempt; if you face a technical disconnection or proctoring issue, report it immediately with screenshots.
- Retake eligibility is typically after 7 days unless an exception is granted by admissions/support.
- Scores and next steps (batch recommendation or foundational prep suggestions) are shared via email within 2–3 business days.
