# Class Schedules, Batches & Attendance

## Batch Timings
- Weekday Evening Batch: Monday to Friday, 7:00 PM – 9:30 PM IST.
- Weekend Batch: Saturday & Sunday, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM IST.
- Doubt-Clearing & Mentor Office Hours: Wednesday and Saturday, 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM IST.

## Recordings & Missed Classes
- All live lectures are recorded and uploaded to the student portal within 24 hours.
- Recordings remain accessible for the full duration of the course plus 6 months after completion.

## Attendance & Holidays
- A minimum of 75% attendance in live sessions or documented catch-up submissions is recommended for course completion certificates.
- Major public holidays have scheduled breaks announced at least 1 week in advance on the portal calendar.
