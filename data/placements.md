# Placement Support & Career Services

## Eligibility for Placement Assistance
- Complete at least 80% of assignments and pass the required capstone/project checkpoints.
- Maintain at least 75% attendance or approved catch-up status.
- Complete a mock technical interview and resume review before referrals begin.

## What Placement Support Includes
- Resume and GitHub/portfolio review workshops.
- 1-on-1 mock technical and behavioral interviews with mentor feedback.
- Curated job openings, interview prep sessions, and referral drives with partner companies.
- Note: Placement assistance provides training, referrals, and interview preparation; job offers depend on candidate performance and hiring demand.
